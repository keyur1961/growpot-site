Why a Queensland-specific guide?

A lot of gardening advice online is written for cool temperate climates in the UK, the US or southern Australia. In the subtropics, the timing is different: coriander prefers our winter, basil loves our summer, and the biggest threat to a potted plant is often a week of storms rather than frost. Growpot keeps things local and honest.
